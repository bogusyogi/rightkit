# Right Suite Cookie and Local Storage Notice

**Version 1.0 · Effective July 16, 2026**

Right Suite marketing sites do not use advertising pixels or cross-site behavioral advertising. Ordinary public pages are designed to work without non-essential cookies.

Checkout, fraud prevention, payment authentication, security, load balancing, and signed-in account features may use cookies or similar browser storage strictly necessary to provide the feature a visitor requests. Stripe, Cloudflare, and the relevant Right Suite store or account service may set or read that storage under their own notices. Blocking necessary storage can prevent checkout, authentication, or security controls from working.

We do not place analytics, personalization, or advertising cookies without first adding an appropriate control and, where required, obtaining consent. If that practice changes, the applicable product Privacy Notice and this notice must be updated before release.

You can delete or block browser storage through browser settings. This does not delete order, license, tax, fraud-prevention, or support records retained on our systems under the applicable Privacy Notice.
