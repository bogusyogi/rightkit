# Right Suite v1.1 focused legal-review checklist

Give this checklist, the master documents, HeardRight schedule/privacy notice, checkout acceptance copy, and one example receipt to the reviewing lawyer. Ask for a fixed-fee verification and line edits, not a new drafting project.

## Highest-priority questions

- Is the two-basis model — individual use (Free/Pro, one natural person, own/controlled devices, own freelance work) vs enterprise use (any use by or for an Organization, on its devices, or that it pays for or directs; per-Seat under an Enterprise Order) — a clear and enforceable dividing line in target markets, including the 30-day cure once enterprise use begins?
- Does the grant preserve the intended “internal use only; no modification, redistribution, resale, hosting, or copying beyond necessary use/backup” while respecting non-waivable US/EU interoperability, backup, transfer, and consumer rights?
- Are Wyoming governing law and business venue appropriate for Damned Ventures LLC, with sufficient consumer carve-outs?
- Are warranty exclusions, the US$25/12-month fee cap, indirect-damage exclusion, and IP/payment carve-outs valid and commercially sensible for consumer and business sales?
- Are termination, key revocation, refund, chargeback, product-lifetime updates, and discontinued online services described without conflicting with public marketing or consumer law?
- Does the clickwrap create adequate proof for anonymous free users and online commercial purchasers without unnecessary personal data?
- Do the Enterprise Order Terms and evidence bundle prove purchased permission and avoid an invasive audit right?

## Privacy and consumer review

- Verify controller identity/contact and whether any representative, DPO, state privacy notice, or jurisdiction-specific addendum is required.
- Verify GDPR/UK GDPR lawful bases, processor/controller roles for licensing, updates, support, model delivery, and BYOK providers; add a DPA/SCCs only where factually required.
- Verify retention periods and rights-response process.
- Confirm the 14-day refund promise and decide whether to preserve withdrawal rights or add a separate express early-performance waiver. The current launch draft preserves the refund and does not add a waiver.
- Check auto-renewal/negative-option rules if a subscription is introduced later; version 1.0 assumes one-time HeardRight purchases.

## Product and IP review

- Confirm “lifetime of product” wording matches every price page, checkout, receipt, and support statement.
- Confirm trademark ownership and entity name/address.
- Review high-impact-use and voice-command risk allocation.
- Review confidentiality and competing-product restrictions for overbreadth.
- Review every final third-party notice, model/font permission, required license text, and attribution separately from the proprietary EULA.

Record the lawyer's name, jurisdiction, review date, exact document hashes, approved changes, unresolved advice, and the new `acceptanceVersion`. Do not mark the launch package “counsel approved” unless that exact byte set was reviewed.
