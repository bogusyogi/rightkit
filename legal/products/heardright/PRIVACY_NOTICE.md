## 2. Data that normally stays on your device

After the required models are installed, default dictation, command parsing, and deterministic cleanup run locally. Damned Ventures LLC does not receive microphone audio or transcript content for default transcription.

The following are normally stored locally: microphone capture used for an active dictation, transcripts and history, vocabulary, snippets, replacements, settings, command activity, screenshots you create, license-acceptance receipt, local diagnostic logs, and the optional usage-telemetry outbox. You control these through the app, operating system, and local app-data deletion. Text pasted or sent into another application is then handled by that application.

The current usage-telemetry option has no network uploader. If enabled, it creates content-free local events only. Turning it off or enabling Privacy Mode deletes or disables the local outbox as implemented. We will update this notice and request a separate opt-in before introducing a telemetry upload.

## 3. Data we receive

### Website and security

Our hosting and security providers process ordinary request data such as IP address, user agent, requested URL, timestamps, region, and security signals. We use this to deliver the site, prevent abuse, diagnose faults, and protect services.

If you join a waitlist or product-update list, we process your email, signup time, referring page, user agent, country derived at signup, and a truncated one-way IP hash used to limit automated abuse. We use the email to send the messages you requested.

The public marketing site does not use advertising pixels or cross-site advertising trackers. Checkout and payment providers may use cookies or similar storage strictly for payment, fraud prevention, security, and session operation under their notices.

### Purchases and legal acceptance

For a purchase or Commercial Order, we process name, email, billing address, country, phone if supplied, product/SKU, price/tax/payment status, order and receipt identifiers, license key, refund/chargeback state, customer messages, and the legal-document IDs/versions/hashes, acceptance statement, authority statement, and server timestamp associated with the order. We do not receive full card numbers from the payment processor.

### License and trial service

For activation, refresh, deactivation, trial, update entitlement, and fraud prevention, the app may send app key/version, license or trial key, a pseudonymous stable device identifier, platform, entitlement state, and ordinary network metadata such as IP address and request time. We do not use this request to collect audio or transcript content.

### Updates and model delivery

Update checks and model/runtime downloads may expose product, app version, platform/architecture, requested artifact, entitlement proof where needed, IP address, user agent, and request time to our API, storage, and delivery providers. They do not intentionally include dictation audio or transcript content.

### Support

We process the messages and files you choose to send to support. Do not send recordings, transcripts, screenshots, logs, or confidential information unless needed. We use support material to resolve the request, protect the service, and maintain an appropriate support record—not for advertising or general AI-model training.

### Optional third-party AI

If you enable cloud AI polish with your own provider key, the app sends data directly from your device to that provider. The payload may include transcript text and available application/window, focused-field, selection, vocabulary, and writing-region context after local secret-shaped filtering. Audio is not sent for polish. Damned Ventures LLC does not proxy or receive that content, but the provider processes it under your account settings, agreement, and privacy notice.

Optional Apple Intelligence polish, where supported, runs through the operating-system feature and is described as on-device by the product. Your operating-system provider's terms apply.

## 7. Retention

- Local app data remains until you delete it, change settings, clear app data, or uninstall in a way that removes application data.
- Waitlist/update-list records remain until unsubscribe/deletion, or up to 24 months after the relevant launch if there is no engagement.
- Orders, invoices, payment/refund records, and checkout acceptance evidence are generally kept for 7 years for tax, accounting, fraud, and dispute needs.
- License/activation records remain while a key is active and generally up to 24 months after expiry, refund, revocation, or last activity, unless an order/dispute record requires longer retention.
- Routine security and delivery logs are generally kept up to 30 days; incident, fraud, or legal-hold records may be kept longer as needed.
- Support records are generally kept for up to 24 months after closure, or longer if part of a purchase, security, dispute, or legal record.

We may keep de-identified aggregate data that no longer identifies a person.

## 9. Automated decisions and children

We do not use HeardRight account, order, license, or support data to make a solely automated decision producing legal or similarly significant effects about you. Speech and AI output is generated for your review and does not make a decision on our behalf.

HeardRight is not directed to children under 13, or a higher minimum age required for independent consent in their location. If you believe a child gave us personal data improperly, contact us.
