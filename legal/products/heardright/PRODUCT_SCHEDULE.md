## 1. Product

HeardRight is a local-first desktop dictation application. Proprietary Software includes the HeardRight user interface, native shell, voice-command orchestration, product-specific local cleanup and routing logic, product assets, and Licensor-created integrations and model conversions, excluding Third-Party Components identified in the Third-Party Notices.

The application is not sold as a medical device, certified accessibility service, official court reporter, emergency system, or guaranteed verbatim transcription service.

## 2. Eligibility: individual and enterprise use

- An individual may use HeardRight's Free features at no charge on devices they own or personally control, including for their own independent or freelance work.
- An individual may buy Pro for their own use on their own or personally controlled devices.
- Enterprise use — any use by or for an Organization, use an Organization pays for or directs, or use on devices an Organization owns, issues, or manages — requires an Enterprise Order with a Seat for each Worker who uses HeardRight, before that use begins.
- Which basis applies is governed by the Individual use and Enterprise use rules in the EULA.

The optional Pro purchase is an individual entitlement; it does not authorize Enterprise use. Buying Pro on an Organization's behalf does not create an Organization license — Enterprise use needs an Enterprise Order.

## 3. Users and devices

**Free scope:** an individual may install on devices they own or personally control. There is no separate numeric device cap for the current Free edition.

**Individual Pro:** one purchased Pro key is for one natural person and may be activated on that person's owned or personally controlled devices. It may not be shared among multiple people or used for Enterprise use.

**Enterprise scope:** the accepted Enterprise Order states the number of Seats, permitted Workers, devices, Affiliates, reassignment, and deployment. An order code, paid receipt/invoice, accepted legal-document record, and any signed order form together prove the purchased permission.

## 4. Editions and pricing

Free currently includes local dictation, local history, vocabulary, snippets, and core cleanup without a license key. Pro currently includes paid entitlements for voice-command and bring-your-own-key AI features; the live feature list controls only if it does not conflict with this schedule.

As of this schedule's effective date, the public individual Pro offer is a one-time **US$149 Founders** license with a **US$199 standard** reference price. The exact price, currency, taxes, edition, and refund terms shown at checkout control that order. Enterprise (per-Seat) pricing requires an Enterprise Order and is not created by purchasing an individual key without written authorization.

No subscription or automatic renewal is included in the current HeardRight desktop offer.

## 6. Local data and storage

Default transcription and command parsing run on the device after required models are present. Microphone audio is not uploaded to Damned Ventures LLC for transcription. Transcripts, history, vocabulary, snippets, settings, command activity, license receipt state, and local logs are stored on the device unless the user takes an export/share action or enables a network-backed feature.

The app may temporarily use the clipboard to paste dictated text and may write screenshots to the clipboard or disk according to the user's setting. Users are responsible for device access, backups, retention, and content entered into other applications.

## 7. Network features

HeardRight may contact network services for:

- paid license activation, refresh, deactivation, trial, and revocation status using app key/version, license or trial key, and a pseudonymous device identifier;
- signed application update checks using product, version, platform, and ordinary connection metadata;
- model/runtime downloads using product/entitlement requests and ordinary CDN connection metadata;
- online checkout, receipts, refunds, support, and commercial-order administration; and
- optional AI polish when deliberately enabled.

The current usage-telemetry switch writes content-free events to a local outbox only; the released app has no telemetry uploader. Before any uploader is introduced, it requires a Privacy Notice update, a separate opt-in control, a documented payload/endpoint/retention rule, and release verification.

## 8. AI polish and third-party providers

Core dictation and deterministic cleanup are local. On supported macOS devices, optional Apple Intelligence polish may run on device under the operating-system provider's terms.

Optional cloud polish uses a provider key the user configures. When enabled, the app may send the transcript and available writing context—such as application/window identity, focused field, selected text, saved vocabulary, and writing region—directly from the device to the chosen provider after locally filtering secret-shaped context. Audio is not sent for polish. Provider availability, accuracy, charges, retention, and training controls are governed by the user's provider account and terms.

Users must not send content to a provider unless they have authority to do so and must configure any provider-side privacy, retention, region, and no-training controls they require.

## 9. Commands and high-impact use

HeardRight may paste text, send keystrokes, launch applications, invoke shortcuts, capture screenshots, and perform enabled computer-control actions. Users must supervise commands and review material output. HeardRight must not be the sole control for a high-impact or safety-critical use described in the AUP.
