## 2. Data that stays on your device

Source media and projects stay on your device. CutRight never uploads footage, and exports and renders are written locally. Damned Ventures LLC does not receive your source media, projects, exports, or renders.

The following are stored locally on your device: source media, projects, exports and renders, models downloaded into the shared Orthic Labs model store, a per-install random device identifier used for model downloads, and, if you add one, your Anthropic API key, which is stored in your operating-system keychain. You control these through the app, your operating system, and local app-data deletion.

CutRight may use the HeardRight speech service running on the same device through a local socket restricted to the current user. Nothing leaves the device for this.

CutRight has no analytics, no telemetry, and no user account.

## 3. Data we receive

### Website and security

Our hosting and security providers process ordinary request data such as IP address, user agent, requested URL, timestamps, region, and security signals. We use this to deliver the site, prevent abuse, diagnose faults, and protect services. The public marketing site does not use advertising pixels or cross-site advertising trackers.

### Purchases and legal acceptance

For a purchase or Commercial Order, we process name, email, billing address, country, phone if supplied, product/SKU, price/tax/payment status, order and receipt identifiers, license key, refund/chargeback state, customer messages, and the legal-document IDs/versions/hashes, acceptance statement, authority statement, and server timestamp associated with the order. We do not receive full card numbers from the payment processor.

### License and trial service

For activation, refresh, deactivation, trial, update entitlement, and fraud prevention, the app may send app key/version, license or trial key, a pseudonymous stable device identifier, platform, entitlement state, and ordinary network metadata such as IP address and request time. We do not use this request to collect media, project, or transcript content.

### Updates and model delivery

Update checks and model/runtime downloads may expose product, app version, platform/architecture, requested artifact, entitlement proof where needed, IP address, user agent, and request time to our API, storage, and delivery providers. They do not intentionally include media, project, or transcript content.

The Parakeet speech model is downloaded from the Orthic model service at heardright-models.adrdsouza.workers.dev; the request is gated by the per-install random device identifier stored on your device. The YuNet face-detection model is downloaded from GitHub (opencv_zoo), which receives the request under its own practices.

### Support

We process the messages and files you choose to send to support. Do not send footage, projects, transcripts, screenshots, logs, or confidential information unless needed. We use support material to resolve the request, protect the service, and maintain an appropriate support record — not for advertising or general AI-model training.

### Optional third-party AI

If you add your own Anthropic API key, the app sends transcript text directly from your device to Anthropic (api.anthropic.com) to score moments for shorts. Media is never sent. Damned Ventures LLC does not proxy or receive that content, but the provider processes it under your account settings, agreement, and privacy notice. Without a key, CutRight uses a local heuristic instead.

### GenRight-generated assets

Generated image, video, voice, avatar, and music assets come through the separately installed GenRight app and its providers. CutRight only imports the files GenRight returns. GenRight's own terms govern its providers.

## 7. Retention

- Local app data remains until you delete it, change settings, clear app data, or uninstall in a way that removes application data.
- Orders, invoices, payment/refund records, and checkout acceptance evidence are generally kept for 7 years for tax, accounting, fraud, and dispute needs.
- License/activation records remain while a key is active and generally up to 24 months after expiry, refund, revocation, or last activity, unless an order/dispute record requires longer retention.
- Routine security and delivery logs are generally kept up to 30 days; incident, fraud, or legal-hold records may be kept longer as needed.
- Support records are generally kept for up to 24 months after closure, or longer if part of a purchase, security, dispute, or legal record.

We may keep de-identified aggregate data that no longer identifies a person.

## 9. Automated decisions and children

We do not use CutRight account, order, license, or support data to make a solely automated decision producing legal or similarly significant effects about you. Speech and AI output is generated for your review and does not make a decision on our behalf.

CutRight is not directed to children under 13, or a higher minimum age required for independent consent in their location. If you believe a child gave us personal data improperly, contact us.

No system is perfectly secure. If a personal-data breach occurs, we will investigate and notify regulators and affected people when and within the time applicable law requires; we do not promise that every event triggers a 72-hour user notice.
