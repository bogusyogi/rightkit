# Right Suite Enterprise Data Processing Addendum Template

**Template version 1.0 · July 16, 2026**

Use this addendum only when a Commercial Order makes Damned Ventures LLC a processor of personal data on behalf of a customer. Do not promise or sign it for local-only processing, direct customer-to-BYOK-provider processing, or ordinary controller activities such as billing, license administration, security, and support unless the actual data flow requires it.

The completed DPA must identify the parties, Commercial Order, subject matter, duration, nature and purpose of processing, data subjects, data categories, documented instructions, confidentiality, security measures, subprocessors, international-transfer mechanism, assistance, deletion/return, audit method, and incident-notification contacts. It must be completed from the current service architecture and subprocessor list; an uncompleted template is not an executed DPA.

Damned Ventures must not process customer personal data as processor beyond documented instructions unless required by law; must ensure authorized personnel are bound by confidentiality; implement appropriate technical and organizational measures; apply lawful subprocessor terms; reasonably assist with data-subject, security, breach, DPIA, and regulator obligations; delete or return processor data after the service as agreed; and make information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance available subject to confidentiality, security, and proportionate audit limits.

For restricted international transfers, the parties must incorporate the transfer mechanism actually applicable to them (for example, the then-current EU Standard Contractual Clauses and UK addendum where required). Merely referring to a DPA or Privacy Notice does not create a transfer mechanism.

This file is an operational template, not a promise that Damned Ventures currently acts as processor or offers every listed enterprise service. Complete and verify it for the actual order before signature.
