# Right Suite Enterprise Order Terms

**Version 1.0 · Effective July 16, 2026**

These Enterprise Order Terms apply when Damned Ventures LLC (**Licensor**) accepts a Commercial Order for a Right Suite desktop product from an Organization (**Customer**). The Right Suite EULA definitions apply.

## 1. Forming and proving an order

A Commercial Order is accepted when (a) both parties sign an order form, (b) Customer completes an online commercial checkout and Licensor issues an order code or paid invoice, or (c) Licensor expressly accepts Customer's purchase order and begins fulfillment. A quote alone is not acceptance.

The accepted order form, invoice/receipt, payment record, order code, and the legal-document versions recorded with the order together prove the permission purchased. A Customer purchase order is administrative only; conflicting or additional boilerplate on it is rejected unless Licensor expressly signs the specific amendment.

The person accepting represents that they are authorized to bind Customer and that the legal name, Affiliates, Seat count, users/devices, billing information, and tax information supplied are accurate.

## 2. Scope

The Commercial Order must identify the product, edition/features, number of Seats and licensed Workers, authorized devices or deployment unit, order term, update/support term, fees, currency, and effective date. Customer may use the Software only within that scope and for internal business operations.

Unless the order expressly says otherwise:

- permission is limited to one legal Customer and its listed Affiliates;
- only Customer's Workers may use the Software;
- seats may be reassigned between Workers when employment or duties change, but may not be shared concurrently;
- contractors may use a seat only while performing services for Customer and only under Customer's control;
- Customer is responsible for its Workers, devices, keys, and contractors; and
- acquisition of an Affiliate does not automatically expand the purchased quantity.

## 3. Seat certification and usage changes

Customer certifies its licensed Seat count and Affiliate list as of the order date. Once per 12-month period, and additionally after reasonable evidence of a material scope breach, Licensor may request a written certification of the current number of Workers using the Software, Affiliates, and deployed devices, so that Seats match actual use. Customer will respond within 15 business days and, if use exceeds licensed Seats, will true up the additional Seats.

Licensor has no right under these terms to inspect unrelated systems, recordings, transcripts, documents, or employee content. Any broader audit requires a separately signed agreement and reasonable confidentiality, scope, timing, and cost protections.

If Customer exceeds purchased scope, the parties will first attempt a good-faith true-up. Customer has 30 days after notice to buy the additional permission or reduce deployment. Deliberate concealment, key sharing, redistribution, or circumvention remains a material breach.

## 4. Fees, taxes, and payment

Fees are due as stated in the Commercial Order and are non-cancellable and non-refundable except as the order, Refund Policy, or mandatory law provides. Customer is responsible for sales, use, VAT, GST, withholding, and similar transaction taxes, excluding taxes on Licensor's net income. If law requires withholding, Customer will provide valid documentation and cooperate on available relief.

Overdue undisputed amounts may accrue the lesser of 1% per month or the maximum lawful rate. Licensor will give notice and at least 10 business days to cure before suspending paid entitlements for non-payment. Customer must raise a good-faith invoice dispute before the due date where reasonably possible and timely pay undisputed amounts.

## 5. Updates and support

Update, maintenance, and support commitments exist only if stated in the Product Schedule or Commercial Order. Security and compatibility patches do not create a professional-services or service-level commitment. Preview, beta, or evaluation features may change or end at any time and are not for production reliance unless the order says otherwise.

## 6. Data and security

The Product Schedule and Privacy Notice describe product data flows. Local recordings, transcripts, vocabulary, and documents are Customer content. A data processing addendum is required only where Licensor processes personal data on Customer's behalf beyond ordinary account, licensing, security, support, and transaction administration. Standard Contractual Clauses are not automatically incorporated; any required transfer mechanism belongs in that signed data processing addendum.

Customer controls its devices, provider keys, content, retention settings, user access, and lawful notices/consents. Licensor will not request Customer content merely to verify a license.

## 7. Confidentiality

Each party will use the other's non-public business, technical, security, and pricing information only to perform the order, protect it with reasonable care, and disclose it only to personnel and advisers who need it and are bound to protect it. Exclusions and compelled-disclosure rules in the EULA apply. This section survives for three years after the order; trade secrets remain protected while legally qualifying as trade secrets.

## 8. Warranties and remedies

Each party warrants it has authority to enter the order. Licensor warrants for 30 days after first delivery that the unmodified Software will materially conform to its then-current documentation on a supported system. Customer's exclusive contractual remedy is, at Licensor's choice, reasonable correction/replacement or refund of the affected license fee and termination of that affected permission. The EULA's warranty disclaimer and liability limits otherwise apply.

## 9. Term and termination

The order term and license duration are stated in the Commercial Order. Either party may terminate an order for an uncured material breach after 30 days' written notice, or immediately if the other party becomes insolvent where law permits. Termination effects follow the EULA. Accrued payment obligations survive.

## 10. Priority and changes

An accepted Commercial Order controls its identified commercial fields and expressly negotiated amendments. The Product Schedule then controls product-specific terms, these Enterprise Order Terms control order administration, and the EULA controls use of the Software. A change is effective only in a writing or authenticated online order record accepted by authorized representatives of both parties.
