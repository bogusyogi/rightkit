# RightApp Privacy Notice

**Notice version 1.0 · Effective October 24, 2026**  
**Controller:** Damned Ventures LLC

**Review status:** this is the unmodified text of the Right Suite desktop template. It has not been reviewed by counsel. An application replaces this notice with its own, and has it reviewed, before it is released.

## What RightApp stores

RightApp stores its settings, its job records and its diagnostic logs on your device, in the application data folder of your user account. A device identifier is kept in your operating system's credential store so the app can recognise the same installation.

## What RightApp sends

RightApp as described in this notice makes no network requests and sends nothing to Damned Ventures LLC.

## Diagnostic logs

Diagnostic logs are written locally with a closed list of fields and without free text. They leave your device only if you choose to send a support bundle.

## Your choices

You can delete the application data folder and the stored device identifier at any time by uninstalling RightApp and removing its folder.

## Changes

The notice is updated whenever the app's behavior changes, and the effective date above changes with it.
