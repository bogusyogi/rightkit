- create, distribute, or facilitate malware, credential theft, phishing, fraud, impersonation, stalking, harassment, threats, exploitation, or unlawful discrimination;
- evade sanctions, export controls, court orders, access restrictions, or other legal duties; or

## 2. Attack people, devices, or services

- obtain unauthorized access to an account, device, network, model, API, key, data, or service;
- discover, exploit, conceal, or distribute a vulnerability for malicious purposes;
- cause denial of service, destructive automation, resource exhaustion, spam, or abusive traffic;
- use voice commands, screenshots, keystrokes, clipboard actions, scripts, or integrations to perform an unlawful or unauthorized computer action; or

Do not use the Software as the sole or unsupervised basis for emergency response, medical diagnosis or treatment, legal advice or filings, financial trading or credit, employment or housing decisions, biometric identification, law-enforcement action, operation of vehicles or industrial equipment, or another use where an error could cause death, personal injury, unlawful discrimination, loss of liberty, or substantial property damage.

Human review is required for important transcripts, commands, and generated text.

Good-faith security research conducted within applicable law is not malicious use. Report a vulnerability privately to security@damnedventures.com and avoid privacy violations, service disruption, data destruction, social engineering, and public disclosure before a reasonable remediation period.
