# rightkit-autoedit

Automatic editing for Right Suite video apps.

- `cuts`: transcript-driven cut plans (retakes, fillers, silences, padding), realised against a timeline, with editing-style measurement.
- `editorial`: the editorial engine over cut plans.
- `variants`: named cut variants of one source, kept as separate timelines, with promotion and compare renders.
- `rules`: scoped editing rules (project, platform, content type, channel) and their resolution matrix with provenance.

Moved into RightKit from CutRight (`cutright-cuts`, `cutright-editorial`, `cutright-variants`, `cutright-rules`) on 2026-11-01.
