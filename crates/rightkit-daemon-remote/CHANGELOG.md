# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (unpublished)

- New crate. The remote side of the daemon moved here from `rightkit-daemon` 0.6.0, where it was three modules behind three features: `remote` (the daemon-api v1 HTTPS host: `Daemon`, enrolment and pairing, signed device routes, uploads, OAuth callback and signed webhook routes, durable state and events, leased tasks over `rk-link/1`, public routes, `DeviceCalls` for `POST /v1/call/{method}`, `router`, `serve`, `serve_blocking`; and with `link` alone the device client, signing and pairing initiator), `link` (`rk-link/1` frames and the blocking `LinkClient`) and `relay` (`rk-relay/1`: `RelayCore`, `RelaySession`, tickets). The 2026-11-11 shape review (docs/audits/2026-11-11-shape-matrix/reports/family-foundation-runtime.md, rightkit-daemon section) judged rightkit-daemon, which every desktop app adopts, the wrong shape because it was several products in one crate; its action: "Split remote/link/relay into their own crate, leaving local service + loopback."
- Paths: `rightkit_daemon::remote::X`, `rightkit_daemon::link::X` and `rightkit_daemon::relay::X` are now `rightkit_daemon_remote::remote::X`, `rightkit_daemon_remote::link::X` and `rightkit_daemon_remote::relay::X`. The `daemon-api` v1 schema (`schemas/`) and the `remote_budget` bench moved too.
- Features: `link`, `relay` (implies `link`) and `remote` (implies `link`), with the same dependencies they had in rightkit-daemon (`remote` adds `rightkit-child-process` for `DaemonStatus::process`). No feature is on by default. The crate depends on `rightkit-daemon` 0.7.0 (no features) for `contract::{MethodTable, Handler, Caller, new_context, ...}`, `ServiceError` and `write_atomic`.
- `ServiceBuilder::remote` and `remote_with` and `Service::daemon` stayed behind (rightkit-daemon's builder now hosts only locally). Their equivalent is `Daemon::open(cfg, clock, oauth)?.with_calls(DeviceCalls::new(table, handler))` beside the local `Service`, with the same table and handler.
- Runtime class: `continuous = "held-resource"` (`serve_blocking` until `StopHandle::stop`; `LinkClient::start` until its `LinkHandle` drops), measured at app adoption. rightkit-daemon's unmeasured `remote` runtime profile and its CI qualification workload are not carried.
- No behaviour change.
