# KWS onset estimators (rightkit-asr)

Owner: the HeardRight KWS lane (Adrian assigned the KWS objective; Fab2 disclaimed rightkit-asr; the Codex chat is advisory only). Issue: rightkit/rightkit#1273. Status: source only until the owner-approved compile, test and replay run.

## D4 fricative onset estimator

Default off, diagnostic. `OnsetConfig.fricative` (`FricativeParams`: window 250 ms, floor 500 ms, rise 1.0, minimum run 40 ms, vowel window 60 ms, tilt margin 1.0, every value settable) adds `KeywordOnset.fricative`; it never replaces `emission`, `aligned`, `posterior` or `acoustic`. A hiss run counts only when a vowel entry starts within one frame after it and inside the vowel window before the anchor (a temporal-association heuristic, not proof of the same occurrence); a run shorter than the minimum is dropped before ambiguity is counted. It abstains with a named reason (`invalid_evidence`, `floor_short`, `before_epoch`, `no_transition`, `run_too_short`, `ambiguous_transitions`, `window_limit`) and never fabricates a boundary. `KwsSwitches.fricative_strict` (default off) returns no boundary when D4 has no candidate instead of falling back to emission. Qualification is by native replay on the frozen scorer against the matched baseline in the same binary; the owner corpus is evaluation only.

## Firing trace (default off)

Owner: the HeardRight KWS lane (issue rightkit#1533). Status: source only, uncompiled and untested until the owner-approved compile and replay. Purpose: observe, in the real build, the beam facts the offline port could not recover (the firing's decoder context and path, which hypotheses were alive, what was pruned or expired, and why a confirm stream did not fire). It is pure observation: nothing it records is read back by the decoder, so beam order, pruning, `ys_probs`, timestamps and firing cannot depend on it.

Switch: `KwsSwitches.trace: Option<KwsTraceConfig>` (`None` is the default); `rightkit_asr::kws::KeywordSpotterConfig.trace: Option<TraceConfig>` underneath. With it off a stream holds no trace state: the decoder pays one `Option` check per chunk and one `bool` check per step, allocates nothing, and every existing report field and replay row is unchanged. The only text change with it off is the load log line, whose `switches=` Debug gains `trace: None`.

Records, each bounded in count with a dropped counter instead of silent omission, and bounded in bytes: every path history (`ys`, `timestamps`, `ys_probs`) in a firing, hypothesis, top, or merge record keeps only its last `history_tokens` entries (default 16, `--trace-history`), with the original lengths and a `truncated` flag beside it.
- `FiringTrace` (wake and confirm streams; `firing: true`): crate version, keyword and config hashes (FNV-1a 64, with the byte lengths of the three model files; not a model content hash), occurrence counter, keyword; the clocks (`TraceClock`: reset epoch, the feature-frame origin of the current epoch on the clock of `KeywordResult::frames`, feature frames ready, the encoder-consumed extent, samples handed to the stream; these are input counters, never recognition wall time); and the firing at the exact pre-reset event, snapshotted at the firing step before a later hit in the same chunk can overwrite the result: `Firing.context`, the selected path's `ys`, `timestamps`, `ys_probs`, `fired_frame`, `num_trailing_blanks`, score, threshold, and the selected plus up to three other hypotheses alive. At most 16 are held between drains.
- `StepTrace` (confirm stream; `window_frames` above 0): one record per decoded encoder frame from `window_frames` before the wake candidate's first token to `window_frames` after its end (on the confirm stream's own frame clock), up to `capacity_steps` between drains (default 512; later steps are counted in `steps_dropped`). Per step: hypotheses scored and hypotheses after the merge, whether TTL expiry emptied the beam, the top hypothesis (keyword-prefix progress, first-token frame, trailing blanks, token probabilities), the keyword check on it when its state completes a keyword (mean token score against the threshold, and the trailing-blank condition), whether it fired, the paths the active-path TTL dropped (with each one's first-token frame), the keyword paths pruned (none of their children entered the top-k beam), the merges on keyword paths (the timestamps and probabilities each side held, and which side survived), and the keyword-extension candidates: for each parent hypothesis (keyword parents first, then the root, whose extensions are first-token candidates) the tokens that advance it along a keyword, each with its token probability, its selection score, its rank (candidates with a strictly greater score this step; ties unbroken), whether it entered the top-k beam, the beam's cutoff (the lowest selected score), and the parent's best other selected child (token and score). At most eight extensions are recorded per step and the rest are counted in `extensions_omitted`; the whole vocabulary is never logged. Extensions are the parent's direct graph arcs; fail transitions are not followed, and the scores are the selection scores before the keyword boost, so a boost that would have reordered children is not visible. `pruned` still means only that no child of that parent entered the beam; the extension records are what explain an extension that lost while another child survived. The per-step event lists are bounded by the beam width.
- `KwsFeedEvent` (cascade): create, suffix, duplicate, stale, gap, destroy and expire events with the snapshot start, live total, stream origin, samples fed and accepted, pending samples, the cascade reset epoch, and the wake candidate's id, extent and expiry deadline (end plus the confirm timeout).

Surfacing: `Kws::take_trace()` drains `KwsTrace` (stream records collected before any stream was destroyed, plus the feed events); each firing also logs `kws_firing_trace`. `kws_onset_replay --trace`, `--trace-window FRAMES` and `--trace-capacity STEPS` add a `trace` key to each JSONL row (a `TRACE` line in text mode); with none of them the output is unchanged.

Streams: wake and confirm streams are drained on every `take_trace` and before destruction, reset or expiry (a candidate expiring or a gap destroys the confirm stream; its records are kept first). Fresh-confirmation decodes (stage `fresh_confirm`, with the wake candidate's id, collected after the whole decode so a no-fire is recorded) and the idle-wake stream (stage `idle_wake`, collected when it stops or on `take_trace`) are collected too. The replay-only full-command decode and the hey-wake window check create their streams without trace state, so nothing allocates where it cannot be read. The no-fire step window rides the confirm stream. A fresh-confirm stream gets the same window semantics before its first sample is fed: encoder frames around the wake candidate's region on the stream's own clock (the stream starts at the snapshot start; stream `window` is `candidate`). When the snapshot's absolute origin is unknown the stream instead keeps its newest `capacity_steps` steps (older ones are dropped and counted) and its `window` is `unknown_origin`. Provenance: the fresh decode declares its synthetic 200 ms silence tail as padding, so every record's `TraceClock` carries `padding_samples_fed` apart from `samples_fed` (which includes it; real audio fed is the difference, and `samples_fed` is never real-audio latency on a stream given padding), and the fresh stream record carries `real_samples_fed` and `tail_samples_fed` as actually fed before the decode stopped. The feed schedule and stop-on-first-result are unchanged.

Limits: padding provenance is the caller's (the crate cannot tell real from synthetic samples; the trace counts samples fed and nothing about their origin). A history cut to its last entries can drop a keyword's first token from the retained list; `first_token_frame` fields are computed from the full path before the cut. A merge replay under another `merge_timestamps` policy is a counterfactual that needs its own run, not this log. `Hypotheses::add` is unit-tested for identical results with and without the observer (both merge policies, ties, both math paths); a trace-on versus trace-off replay comparison is still owed and belongs after the owner-approved compile. Qualification still needs recognition parity (detections, keywords, frames and probabilities bit-identical on and off on the owner clips and the 373-clip roster) and an overhead run that varies only trace collection.
