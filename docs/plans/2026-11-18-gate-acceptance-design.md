# What a passing gate must prove

Acceptance design, 18 November 2026. Applies to GC-A27 & reset steps 4–8. Adrian’s decision in the RightKit chat on this date supersedes both older cache policies: an expected invalidation runs under the operation’s existing authorization; an unexpected lookup result stops the operation & explains the discrepancy. This document defines the requested behavior. The [companion audit](../../../rustright/docs/audits/loop-2026-11-10/07-acceptance-audit-2026-11-18.md) compares it with current source & the sealed Dell run.

## The verdict

One green release verdict means the named artifact met every applicable, independently checked obligation under the recorded host, broker, inputs, stages & budgets. It is conclusive for that candidate and scope. It supplies no evidence for a different artifact, different host, subsequent regression or property nobody specified. Preserve the verdict as a release input; repeat only the proof whose binding or relevant inputs changed, under the contract’s per-host qualification rules.

Issue a release-eligible receipt only if **all** of these hold:

1. The ordinary launch path ran the selected sealed artifact on the named host. The gate commit, fixture commit, archive identity, installed broker, runner, fixture definitions, budgets & approved requirement revision all match their records. Capture the actual launcher & child identities at runtime.
2. Every required scenario ran or consumed admissible evidence bound to this candidate. Each of NN01–NN42 has a passing assertion, a positive witness that exercised the feature & a validated control showing the judge detects the specified violation. A zero-length sample, missing fixture capability, empty work queue or unavailable instrument yields incomplete evidence. The runner must count what it observed.
3. Every operative contract obligation appears in the coverage inventory. Compare the contract’s identifiers, sub-obligations & approved host/stage applicability against the tested inventory in both directions. The historical 67 are the runner’s inventory; they cannot define by themselves what the contract contains. Split composite obligations where their witnesses differ. Reconcile the separately proposed NN43 explicitly before locking membership.
4. Every applicable obligation has valid evidence, or a separately identified, named waiver within Adrian’s actual authorization. Keep a waiver scoped to the obligation, reason, target & applicable release. Label a waived release as such; only a fully evidenced pass answers “everything met the contract.” A condition applicable on another host needs the matching evidence at its owning boundary. Missing permission, missing fixture or an unimplemented assertion stays blocked. A release receipt enumerates the **complete** expected set & its dispositions so absence cannot masquerade as completion.
5. The last common verifier checks the receipt at each actual publication, upload & trusted installation boundary before the effect. The effect’s selected bytes & origin match the evidence. Staging may happen before qualification. A refusal moves no trusted pointer & sends no upload. A valid candidate must also be able to reach each intended boundary; refusing everything is a broken release route.

Keep the existing gate planner, broker job receipts, coverage, process ledger, host budgets, qualification checks & signed artifact identities. Use the existing runner until its replacement produces the evidence its real consumers require. The [reset plan](2026-11-07-gate-broker-reset.md#7-order-of-work) still sets the order of work.

**Host & commit binding.** A native install or native publication must derive its expected host independently of the submitted receipt. For hosted public publication, name the eligible enrolled qualification origin in the release selection before evaluating receipts. Reconcile that selection with NN30’s “releasing host” wording & existing per-host independence. A hosted CI executor cannot pretend to be the Mac or Dell that supplied native evidence. The verifier must independently join the producing commit in the sealed source record to the release selection, and the actual fixture & runner to their approved revisions. Keep the positive path working for a single authorized origin without depending on another machine. GC-A06’s older wording requires changed stages witnessed on both managed hosts before suite-wide app adoption. Track that cross-host adoption claim separately from a single host’s NN30 qualification or promotion. Only the combined claim requires both results.

## Cache decision before execution

**Forecast which rows should hit before looking up whether they hit.** Otherwise an empty cache could predict zero hits after observing itself empty & claim perfect accuracy. Use the last valid baseline, the selected tree, independently identified change set, row dependency closure, and relevant tool, broker, policy & time-window changes. Preserve a baseline inventory outside the cache entries whose loss is under test. The acceptance fixture has an independently authored expected row set for each controlled edit; reproducing a planner bug in both predictor & grader must fail the fixture check.

1. **Freeze the forecast.** For each logical row, record identity, target, feature set, digest inputs, reuse eligibility, prior trustworthy result (including deterministic failure), expected disposition & evidence for it. Distinguish expected reuse, expected execution & genuinely uncacheable work. Include expected counts per stage and the row identities behind them. A known cold start, new row, declared expiry or planned toolchain change needs explicit prior evidence. A broad “inputs changed” label, an empty store, or a low estimate chosen from observing the current lookup cannot authorize extra work.
2. **Perform a read-only lookup.** Compare the complete actual disposition map with the frozen one, including missing, duplicate or extra rows. Compare identities even when hit counts match. A valid pre-existing result for a previously seen changed input can be forecast as a hit. If observed outcomes differ from the forecast in either direction, stop for reconciliation. No percentage tolerance permits an incorrect row.
3. **On agreement, execute only the forecast misses & prescribed uncacheable work.** An expected invalidation due to a known input change needs no *additional cache approval*. An unchanged deterministic failure remains a cache **hit** and makes the product result fail with its original evidence, with zero matching compiler starts. Keep general authorization for compiling, testing, installing & releasing separate.
4. **On disagreement, fail before dispatching the affected work.** One classified-but-unpredicted miss still fails. Emit a nonzero verdict, leave release ineligible & notify Adrian through the invoking job’s failure path. If a later stage depends on earlier outputs, freeze & compare its forecast as soon as those inputs exist, before dispatching that stage. A concurrent eviction, generation change or different broker identity between lookup & use invalidates the decision. Stop at the first detected divergence; retain any already completed work & record any in-flight work. Never repair the mismatch by silently running the row or rewriting the prediction.
5. **Show the reason someone can verify.** Return host, stage, row ID, prior/current keys & baseline, expected/actual dispositions and counts, changed input identities, previous/current tool or broker fingerprints, store or integrity outcome, source evidence & dispatched-work count. Redact secrets. If a record vanished and evidence cannot distinguish eviction from deletion, report “expected entry absent; removal cause unknown,” keep the job failed & identify what evidence would establish the cause. A new run needs a corrected cause or changed input & a fresh forecast. A narrative explanation cannot turn a failed attempt green.

The existing gate already makes a lookup before dispatch & writes a miss report. Its unexpected-miss branch then falls through to scan execution. The working S11/S17 assertion reconciles reported misses with work that **ran**. Neither implements this decision. Retain their evidence collection, change the dispatch decision & give the grader an independent expectation. [Production lookup](../../packages/release/bin/rightkit-gate.mjs) · [Current acceptance judge](../../packages/release/gate-acceptance.mjs)

Keep separate denominators for gate row reuse & broker/compiler freshness. The earlier GC-A13 daily report describes compiler rebuilds; it cannot satisfy an unexpected-miss stop. Where the gate promises a warm compiler unit inside a legitimately rerun row, bind that forecast to broker generation, target, toolchain & inputs, then compare the signed freshness evidence. If the broker can detect a mismatch at admission, refuse there. If the discrepancy first becomes observable during execution, fail that stage, retain its evidence & dispatch no dependent work. Daily reports may aggregate failures; they cannot downgrade them. A claimed 100% row-hit rate must never stand in for a compiler-level measurement.

### Cache acceptance cases

| Setup on a real gate | Required result |
|---|---|
| Warm unchanged revision; prior successes & deterministic failures available | Exact predicted hits. Zero corresponding scans/compiler starts. The prior failure still fails the product verdict. |
| Single leaf edit, unrelated workspace unchanged | Only proven dependent rows invalidate. Print the affected set, run it under existing authority, keep unrelated rows warm. |
| Known new row or explicitly planned cold fixture; first run then warm repeat | First-run expected misses with their predeclared reasons; second run reuses every eligible result. An unexplained empty store on a previously warm run stops. |
| Relevant compiler or broker change vs irrelevant environment change | Only the precomputed affected set invalidates. Corroborate the changed compiler on the broker that compiled. Irrelevant run/session metadata changes no key. |
| One expected hit is missing; one record has lost integrity; a lookup report is absent | Each stops with its own diagnosis & no work performed for the affected stage. Matching an old filename or providing prose cannot satisfy integrity. |
| One extra miss offset by an extra hit, so aggregate is unchanged | Stop. List both row IDs, expected & actual outcomes. A stale hit after a changed input is as serious as wasted compilation. |
| A group supplies member results; a concurrent writer updates the store | Reuse only with valid group/member identity, inputs & outcome. Pin the decision to a consistent generation; detect unexpected changes before use without duplicating work. |
| A deliberately uncacheable check, a skipped required row, a zero-row sample | Account separately. Only the predeclared uncacheable check runs. A skipped owed row or an empty required sample blocks acceptance. |

## Proving the rest of the contract

Build a single coherent evidence set from the following scenarios. Share snapshots, archived observations & setup between compatible assertions. Keep host identity, timestamps, executor and artifact digests on each evidence reference.

| Scenario | Independent observation that decides pass or fail |
|---|---|
| Warm runs, edits to source, manifest & lockfile, upgrade and resident restart | Known dependency graph & affected row inventory, exact cache forecasts, real file reads/writes, process starts/ends, cache record provenance & zero unexpected work. Compare the actual candidate to the proper predecessor. |
| Real work with serial dependencies & parallel ready work | Fixture offers multiple ready jobs, compatible/incompatible feature sets, multi-target work & docs. Capture ready, queued, started & finished times with actual available lanes and reservations. Measure idle capacity only while work is eligible. Include setup, waits, teardown & host-specific budgets. |
| Multi-unit / multi-workspace compilation | Check exact Class A count; individual/workspace dependencies; compiler, doc & target arguments per invocation; per-member results with correct attribution. A fixture actually requiring both targets & jobs is mandatory before judging grouping. |
| Crash, cancellation, refusal & restart | Normal host mechanism; use the installed broker's durable receipts, process lifetime, lease & snapshot observations. Show the original job is terminal, an unrelated owner survives & the next authorized job succeeds. Check both success and named refusal on the host that owns the operation. |
| Real adapter, package, rendering, installation & QA scenarios | Each applicable platform/stage supplies its actual output and readback, including installed journeys against the selected artifact. Keep install/launch consent explicit. Cross-target stand-ins cannot qualify foreign native behavior. Reuse separately qualified native evidence with exact identity. |
| Release / trusted install and coverage integrity | At every supported boundary exercise genuine valid and denied decisions via its production verifier, with observed no-effect on refusal. Cover incomplete evidence, wrong identity, wrong host, changed bytes, stale candidate, failed job, inapplicable waiver & missing origin. On a separately approved normal promotion/publication, bind the actual effect & readback to that same proof. Do not require a public release merely to exercise a negative case. |

For each judge keep one known-good observation & one **specific** known-bad observation. The bad case must fail the named rule for the intended reason with other prerequisites valid. A broken fixture that stops before the relevant stage proves nothing about that judge. Use existing independently captured ledgers & failed receipts where sufficient; keep supporting unit checks cheap. Reuse the existing proof mode for judge validation where appropriate. It has a distinct verdict from candidate acceptance. A changed judge, fixture, collection path or verifier invalidates the corresponding judge proof.

## Implementation sequence within the reset

1. In step 4, reconcile historical refusals with the exact current candidate. Use the companion audit for investigation. Assign every outstanding obligation a source, positive witness, negative witness, owning subsystem, applicable stage/host & release consumer.
2. Implement the forecast/lookup/dispatch decision in the production gate & its paired tests. Update S11/S17 to distinguish expected invalidation, unchanged failure reuse & unexpected miss refusal. Replace older blanket-stop and blanket-run wording together with the tested change; review the contract/runner pins through their existing lock procedure.
3. Repair the observed false passes & false failures as classes. Fill the 45 missing clause witnesses in their owning suites/layers; wire signed results into the single evidence set. Carry assertions forward into the step 5 replacement; keep existing enforcement until its replacement is accepted by actual callers.
4. Complete the admission path in both directions. Demonstrate that the authentic eligible record is produced, handed to each real consumer & accepted for its artifact, and each named negative refuses before effects. Address the broker & contract replacements in reset steps 6–7 without weakening those checks.
5. In step 8, independently qualify each installed host against the frozen design. Publish a human-readable verdict with coverage, named gaps/waivers & receipt identity. The final success case has 42 evidenced obligations, all operative clauses accounted for, exact forecast reconciliation & end-to-end admission for the same bytes.
